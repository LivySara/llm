# the topic: Today's AI news came out at a breakneck pace

>> breakneck pace
meaning: Extremely fast speed
eg:
- AI is developing at a breakneck pace
- Technology changes at a breakneck pace

>> the core of ...
meaning: the most important part of something
eg:
- Lets focus on the core of the problem

>> officially
meaning: in a formal or confirmed way
eg:
- The company officially announced its new product

>> IPO
meaning: Initial Public Offering. When a private company sells its shares to the public for the first time

>> ticker
meaning: A short symbol used to identify a company's stock
eg:
- Apple's ticker is AAPL

>> price range
meaning: the lowest and highest prices something is expected to have
eg:
- the guided price range is $70-$78

>> with ...
meaning: adds extra information
eg:
- I bought a laptop with 16GB of RAM
- The company launched a product with three new features
